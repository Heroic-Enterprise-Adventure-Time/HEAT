# 0007: Canned actions, not free-text chat

- **Status:** Decided
- **Date:** 2026-10-08

## Decision

Canned messages and actions rather than free text, for example one hero waving at another on the same screen.

## Considered and not chosen

**Free-text chat.** A moderation risk on a public site. Canned actions remove the problem.
